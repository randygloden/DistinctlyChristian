The Concept

Zero-Profit Ministry to spread our faith. No salaries, donated assets, and attention towards putting out shirts, or other item for wear or carry, that show our faith.

Because of our mission and model, we will be low volume, and very selective on messaging and where we distribute these shirts. If we receive enough donations for supplies and equipment, we can expand as God provides!
