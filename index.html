Wear Your Faith

Personal Witness

We testify that our faith is in God alone. We believe that Jesus saved us by Grace, through Faith, and that we owe Him everything. Yet, when we go to Walmart, we wear a shirt that presents the Good News of Coca-Cola, Disney, Under Armour, or God forbid some "celebrity." Tired of promoting things that have no value?

Sending the Gospel beyond our reach

How clever would it be if we could flip the script and provide an alternative message that would take seeds of our faith, slip that message right back into the homes of those we are trying to reach.

In a nutshell, the goal of this ministry is to create clothing or other items that reach the world for Christ at cost, or if funds allow . . . for no cost. But the more clever angle, is our desire to penetrate the homes of our outreach ministries, with free shirts, specifically taylored with the the Gospel. We might not be able to get a foot in the door, but a free shirt containing the Gospel, will pass right through.

The Ministry Structure

Yes, we could use some help. Though we will create our materials at very low cost, there is still a cost, and the more resources we have, the more gospel we can send into homes.

We want to create very polished designs, and could really use a talented graphic artist to create our designs.

With or without funding, we will move forward at whatever rate God provides. This ministry is mostly self-funded on a shoe string budget, with low overhead, minimal assets, and a laser focus on our core mission.

Standards

  • We are KJV By Conviction, so any scripture we quote, will be KJV.
  • We will not flirt with worldly messaging. For example, we will NOT take "This Bud's for you" and switch that to "This Blood's for You."
  • We will not mimick the look of Satans Marketing (HardRock Album/Concert) to deliver our messaging.
  • If a suggestion comes to us that doesn't pass the smell test, it's out.
  • There are many good causes, but we have a message more urgent than politics or charity. Where we succeed, a seed will take root, hearts will change, and minds and politics will follow. There is nothing wrong with political messaging, or finding the cure for cancer, but we will not be distracted from our mission.

  • We will not win the lost by being adversarial. If God's message offends, so be it. But the intent is to plant a seed, not to burn ground . . . except for Satan, whom we hope to tick off every day of our lives!
  • There is nothing wrong shirts that promote a youth group, church camp, Christian school, or other ministry-branded apparel, especially if integrated with Distinctly Christian messaging. However, short runs of custom designs entail a lot of setup time and effort so our priority is to spread precise Gospel Messaging. Since this messaging will stay consistent, after the initial setup, we can produce this content quickly and in volume. If time and resources allow, we might create some custom designs for specific ministries, or assist ministries with DIY DTF strategies and training.

    • Where We Are in Our Efforts

    The space we will use for screen printing is under construction, nevertheless, to get the ball rolling, and to start "learning/experimenting" we started producing some shirts using DTF, and have our first proof of concept shirts delivered to Mercy Baptist Church in Manchester, TN. Though a bit more expensive per shirt than Screen Printing, for low volume proof of concept, or if time permits, custom design ministry shirts, we can do limited runs with DTF (Direct to Film), using affordable vendors for the film creation and then using a heat press to apply. Despite all the DTF buzz . . . DTF will have a vinyl feel, it just does. It's also more expensive, though not terrible. DTF is much faster to setup and capable of full color output, so there are pros and cons.

    Though we are currently building out our facilities for Screen Printing (high volume), we already have a 6-color screen press, washout booth, exposure unit, screens, large-ish format printer (14"), flash dryer, conveyor dryer, some plastisol ink, and misc. tools for use as long as we need them.

    Our setup will be extremely modest with an efficient space to house this equipment and serve as the home for this endeavor.
