Wear Your Faith

We testify that our faith is in God alone, we believe that Jesus saved us by Grace, through Faith, and that we owe him everything. Yet, when we go to Walmart, we wear a shirt that presents the Good News of CocaCola, Disney, UnderArmor, or God forbid some "Celebrity." Tired of advertising for the wrong team (or no team), I sought to up my witness efforts and explored my options at Amazon and other places, and it quickly became obvious that money, not ministry, was the motivatation for these products. If I wanted to buy 100 shirts to distribute to our youth outreach, it would cost a small fortune.

But the need goes deeper than just my own efforts, if you pay attention to youth outreach efforts, many on these children are basically wearing walking billboards for Satan. How clever would it be if we could flip the script, and provide an alternative message that would take the message of Jesus back into their homes and shine a light where there is currently none. Great idea? But for that to work, $29 for an off the shelf Amazon shirt isn't going to cut it. What if I could deliver "Distinctly Christian" messaging on quality shirts for $3/each give or take? I'm pretty sure that price would would open a whole world of opportunity to spread the word

The Concept

Zero-Profit Mission to spread our faith through messaging on the items you wear and carry. To make our vision work, we will focus our attention towards putting out quality shirts/wearables/carryables, that show our faith at the lowest cost possible.

Because of our mission and model, and the financial limits that imposes on us, we will be very selective on messaging and where/how we distribute these shirts. We can expand as God provides, but ultimately, we want to keep our cost super low to spread the word as far possible.

What Message Exactly?

And this is where I lose some of you . . . . If I do direct4 scripture quotes, I "WILL NOT" use corrupt translations. If you have an issue with the KJV, I'll save you some time, and tell you this ministry isn't for you. We will not flirt with worldly messaging to send our message, for example, we NOT take "This Buds for you", and switch that to "This Bloods for You". We will however print messaging such as: A man is not fool who gives what he cannot keep to gain what he cannot lose ---Jim Elliot

Where we are in our Efforts

We have use of a 6 color screen press/washout booth/exposure unit/screens/large-ish format printer (14")/flashdryer/conveyor dryer/screens/plastisol ink/misc tools for use as long as we need them. We are building out a small/effient space to house this equipment and serve as the home for this endeavor. For limited runs, we can do DTF, outsourcing the film creation, then using a heatpress to apply.

We are not Tax Exempt

This ministry concept is radical, unproven, self funded, and seemingly nuts! We will spend some time looking at the Tax Exempt logistics, but ultimately, we have to prove the concept, and get the first examples out the door. There are incredible possibilites here, but again, we need to prove it first.
