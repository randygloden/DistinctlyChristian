DistinctlyChristian.org

The Concept

Zero-Profit Ministry to spread our faith. No salaries, donated assets, and attention towards putting out shirts, or other item for wear or carry, that show our faith.

Because of our mission and model, we will be low volume, and very selective on messaging and where we distribute these shirts. If we receive enough donations for supplies and equipment, we can expand as God provides!

Where we are in our Efforts

We have use of a 6 color screen press/washout booth/exposure unit/screens/large format printer/flashdryer/conveyor dryer/screens/plastisol ink/misc tools for use as long as we need them. We are building out a small/effience space to house this equipment and serve as the home for this ministry. For limited runs, we can do DTF, outsourcing the film creation, then using a heatpress to apply.
