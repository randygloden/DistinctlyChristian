Wear Your Faith

Personal Witness

Many people that will not read a tract, will read a shirt.

Everywhere I go, I see people wearing clothing that preach the Good News of Coca-Cola, Disney, Under Armour, or God forbid some "celebrity." There are occassional shirts that have a "religious" meaning, but very few designed to reach the lost. Satan is spreading his message very effectively... let's not let Satan go unchecked.

In a nutshell, we intend to spread the Gospel, and promote Christian values via two primary strategies:

  1. Inject the gospel in the homes of the unsaved via Messaging on Apparel or other items. We will create strategic messaging to insert the gospel where we can not get a foot in the door. These items would be free for bus kids, or other outreach.
  2. Create Messaging that Christians can afford, and to be a light to the community. We are 100% administration free, so we supply for cost of materials, or if we have ample donations, below cost or free.

The Ministry Structure

All workers for this ministry are volanteer, so every penny goes into the physical needs of the ministry itself. We are taking stewardship to the next level! If someone donates, that donation will go to tools, materials, or shipping. Will we need funding? Yes, we could use some help. Though we will create our materials at very low cost, there is still a cost to purchase inks, garments, other items to print, etc. Initially, we will focus on local ministries, but if we grow, in some cases, we may need shipping. Hopefully ... local Churches or individuals that believe in our cause will donate to offset the cost of what we provide to them and perhaps a bit more to help fund further outreach.

Other Needs: Beyond funding, and perhaps more importantly, we could really use a volanteer talented graphic artist to create designs.

Standards

  • Any scripture we quote in English, will be KJV.
  • If a suggestion comes to us that doesn't pass the smell test, it's out.
  • There are many good causes, but we have a message more urgent than politics or charity. Where we succeed, a seed will take root, hearts will change, and minds and politics will follow. There is nothing wrong with political messaging, or finding the cure for cancer, but we will not be distracted from our mission.
  • We will not win the lost by being adversarial. If God's message offends, so be it. But the intent is to plant a seed, not to burn ground . . . except for Satan, whom we hope to tick off every day of our lives!
  • There is nothing wrong with apparel that promote a Specific Church, Youth Group, Church Camp, Christian School, or other ministry-branded apparel, especially if integrated with Distinctly Christian messaging. However, short runs of custom designs entail a lot of setup time and effort so our priority is to spread precise Gospel Messaging. Since this messaging will stay consistent, after the initial setup, we can produce this content quickly and in volume. If time and resources allow, we may create some custom designs for specific ministries, or assist ministries with DIY DTF strategies and training.

    • Where We Are in Our Efforts

    The space we will use for screen printing is under construction, nevertheless, to get the ball rolling, and to start "learning/experimenting" we have started producing some shirts using DTF, and have our first proof of concept shirts delivered to Mercy Baptist Church in Manchester, TN. Though a bit more expensive per shirt than Screen Printing, for low volume proof of concept, or if time permits, custom design ministry shirts, we can do limited runs with DTF (Direct to Film), using affordable vendors for the film creation and then using a heat press to apply. Despite all the DTF buzz . . . DTF will have a vinyl feel, it just does. It's also more expensive, though not terrible. DTF is much faster to setup and capable of full color output, so there are pros and cons.

    Though we are currently building out our facilities for Screen Printing (high volume), we already have a 6-color screen press, washout booth, exposure unit, screens, large-ish format printer (14"), flash dryer, conveyor dryer, some plastisol ink, and misc. tools for use as long as we need them.

    Our setup will be extremely modest with an efficient space to house this equipment and serve as the home for this endeavor.
