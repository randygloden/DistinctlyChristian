Wear Your Faith

So, we testify that our faith is in God alone, we believe that Jesus saved us by Grace, through faith, and that we owe him everything. Yet, when we go to Walmart, we wear a shirt that presents the Good News of CocaCola, Disney, UnderArmor, or God forbid some "Celebrity." Hmmmm, we want to do better so we go to amazon to search for something better to present to those that cross our paths, and find a few options that look decent, but are cost prohibitive.

We buy a few and sunday morning, we notice our youth outreach efforts are bringing children that are basically wearing walking billboards for Satan. How clever would it be if we could provide an alternative that would take the message of Jesus back into their homes and shine a light where there is currently none. Great idea? But for that to work, $29 for an off the shelf Amazon shirt isn't going to cut it.

The Concept

Zero-Profit Mission to spread our faith through messaging on the items you wear and carry. To make our vision work, we will focus our attention towards putting out quality shirts/wearables/carryables, that show our faith at the lowest cost possible.

Because of our mission and model, and the financial limits that imposes on us, we will be very selective on messaging and where/how we distribute these shirts. We can expand as God provides, but ultimately, we want to keep our cost super low to spread the word as far possible.

Where we are in our Efforts

We have use of a 6 color screen press/washout booth/exposure unit/screens/large-ish format printer (14")/flashdryer/conveyor dryer/screens/plastisol ink/misc tools for use as long as we need them. We are building out a small/effient space to house this equipment and serve as the home for this endeavor. For limited runs, we can do DTF, outsourcing the film creation, then using a heatpress to apply.

We are not Tax Exempt

This ministry concept is radical, unproven, self funded, and seemingly nuts! We will spend some time looking at the Tax Exempt logistics, but ultimately, we have to prove the concept, and get the first examples out the door. There are incredible possibilites here, but again, we need to prove it first.
