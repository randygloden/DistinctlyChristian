Wear Your Faith

We testify that our faith is in God alone, we believe that Jesus saved us by Grace, through Faith, and that we owe him everything. Yet, when we go to Walmart, we wear a shirt that presents the Good News of CocaCola, Disney, UnderArmor, or God forbid some "Celebrity." Tired of advertising for the wrong team? I sought to up my witness efforts and explored my options at Amazon and other places, and it quickly became evident that money, not ministry, was the motivatation for these products.

Take notice what our youth are wearing, many of these children are basically wearing walking billboards for Satan. How clever would it be if we could flip the script, and provide an alternative message that would take seeds of our faith, and send that right back into their homes and provide light where there is currently none. Great idea? But for that to work at scale, we need to reduce the cost dramatically, and provide our apparel to this group at zero cost. What if I could deliver "Distinctly Christian" messaging on quality shirts for $3/each give or take? I'm pretty sure that price would open a whole world of opportunity to spread the word. For $100, I could send a lot of shirts home via Church bus ministries and youth groups.

The Ministry Structure

Zero-Profit Mission to spread our faith through messaging on the items people wear and carry. To make our vision work, we will focus our attention towards putting out quality shirts/wearables/carryables, that show our faith at the lowest cost possible.

Because of our mission and model, and the financial limits that imposes on us, we will be very selective on messaging and where/how we distribute these shirts. We can expand as God provides, but ultimately, we want to keep our cost super low to spread the word as far possible. Be want to be vigilent that our efforts are not compromised by bad actors, looking to take advantage.

What Message Exactly?

And this is where I lose some of you . . . . For items with scripture quotes, I "WILL NOT" use corrupt translations. If you have an issue with the KJV Only Stance, you are not my enemy, but I'll save you some time, and tell you we will not budge on that issue.

We will not flirt with worldly messaging, for example, we NOT take "This Buds for you", and switch that to "This Bloods for You." If a suggestion comes to us that doesn't pass the smell test, it's out. We will however print messaging such as: A man is no fool who gives what he cannot keep to gain what he cannot lose ---Jim Elliot

There are many good causes, but we have a message more urgent than politics or charity. Where we succeed, a seed will take root, hearts will change and minds will follow.

We don't do Hate. If God's message offends, so be it. But the intent is to plant a seed, not to burn ground . . . except for Satan, whom I hope to tick off every day of my life!

There is nothing wrong with Youth Group, Church Camp, Christian School, or other Ministry branded apparel, especially if blended with Distinctly Christian messaging, however, short runs of custom designs entail a lot of setup effort and cost, and we have limited time and resources. Over time, if we attract enough help, we might do some of this, or assist ministries with DIY DTF strategies and training.

Where we are in our Efforts

We have use of a 6 color screen press/washout booth/exposure unit/screens/large-ish format printer (14")/flashdryer/conveyor dryer/screens/plastisol ink/misc tools for use as long as we need them. We are building out a small/efficient space to house this equipment and serve as the home for this endeavor. For Proof of Concept, we can do limited runs, with DTF, using affordable vendors for the film creation, then using a heatpress to apply.

We are not Tax Exempt

We will spend some time looking at the Tax Exempt logistics, but our priority is to get up and running, and to get the first examples out the door.

Are we Nuts?

This ministry concept is radical, unproven, mostly self funded, and yes, with the investment already made, it feels a little nuts! But I see incredible possibilites for this type of ministry, and with Gods help, will prove it! I believe God is in this, and this ministry will absolutely require God's blessing to succeed!
